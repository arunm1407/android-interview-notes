# Tosca Hands-on Exercises

> **8 labs** from first scan to CI/CD. Each exercise has objectives, prerequisites, steps, validation criteria, and extension challenges. Track completion on the [Printable Checklist]({{< relref "../tosca-checklist" >}}).

| # | Exercise | Level | Time | Topics |
|---|----------|-------|------|--------|
| 1 | [First Scan & TestCase]({{< relref "01-first-scan-and-testcase" >}}) | Junior | 2–3 hr | Scan, Module, TestCase, F8 run |
| 2 | [Buffer & Document Chain]({{< relref "02-buffer-and-chain" >}}) | Junior | 3 hr | Status bar buffer, multi-TC chain |
| 3 | [Table Line Items]({{< relref "03-table-line-items" >}}) | Mid | 3–4 hr | Steering params, multi-row VA01 |
| 4 | [Recovery Scenarios]({{< relref "04-recovery-scenarios" >}}) | Mid | 2–3 hr | Popups, credit block, system messages |
| 5 | [TestSheet Data-Driven]({{< relref "05-testsheet-data-driven" >}}) | Mid | 2–3 hr | Excel import, 10 iterations |
| 6 | [API + GUI Hybrid]({{< relref "06-api-hybrid-odata" >}}) | Mid | 4–5 hr | OData POST, GUI verify |
| 7 | [Framework Refactor]({{< relref "07-framework-refactor" >}}) | Senior | 6–8 hr | Component library, layers |
| 8 | [CI/CD Pipeline]({{< relref "08-cicd-pipeline" >}}) | Senior | 4–6 hr | Git, Tosca CI, Jenkins |

**Sandbox requirements:** SAP DEV or QAS with VA01, VL01N, VF01, ME21N access; Tosca Commander + Engine; test customer and material master data.

**Diagrams:** See [Architecture Diagrams]({{< relref "../diagrams" >}}) for visual reference during labs.
